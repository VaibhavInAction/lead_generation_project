## Patterns these examples teach

**Request phrasings beyond "looking for a X":**
- looking to partner with / want to partner with / partner with us
- looking for agency partners / the right agency partner / growth partner
- Calling all [agencies]
- planning to appoint
- on the lookout for
- looking out for agencies
- plural targets: "looking for agencies", "looking for branding agencies"

**"Hiring" here means hiring an AGENCY, not staff** — `We're Hiring: Performance
Marketing Agency`, `#AgencyHiring`, `#HiringAnAgency`, `#Hiring` + agency target.
The job-posting rule must never fire when the object being hired is an
agency/partner/vendor.

**Call-to-action signals:** DM me your case studies · email your portfolio ·
share your company profile · send your pitch deck · tag them in the comments ·
share case studies in the comments · mail us at

**Client industries seen:** real estate, F&B/hospitality, retail, D2C/e-commerce,
nutraceutical/wellness, SaaS/AI, fintech, healthcare, B2B/industrial, engineering.
